# I use IBM Domino email server, some emails are missing!

Modified on Wed, 14 Jun, 2023 at 10:30 AM

IBM Domino email server does not fully implement the IMAP protocol, resulting in certain issues such as problems with period selection. To overcome this in Automatic Email Manager, it is recommended to configure the account to retrieve all emails instead of specifying a specific period, such as the last 7 days.

## 1\. Edit the account in Automatic Email Manager and move to step 3

Proceed to click on 'More options'. On the right side, you can specify the type of emails you wish to receive: either only unread messages or all emails. We recommend selecting 'Unread'. Below that, you can adjust the time period for retrieval, which is initially set to 7 days. Change it to 'Get All emails'.

With these adjustments, your Automatic Email Manager should function smoothly with your Domino server.
